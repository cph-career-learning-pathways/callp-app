User Manual

A quick guide to exploring collections and discovering career-learning resources.

Getting started

  1. Browse learning collections from the home page.
  2. Use the search bar and category filters to narrow your results.
  3. Open a collection to see its description and related resources.

Finding resources

Switch the search mode between Collections and Web to explore different kinds of resources.

For broader results, visit Web Resource Discovery.

Your account

Create an account or log in using the Account menu in the top bar.

If you forget your password, use the reset link on the login page.

Support

Visit Contact Us for available support information or view the Feedback page.
